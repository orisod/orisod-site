What does this tool do?

This tool shows every page of your PDF as a thumbnail you can drag into a new order, or delete entirely, before saving a rebuilt document. The underlying PDF content stays exactly as it was — text remains selectable, only the page order and count change.

When should you use it?

  • Pages in a scanned document came out in the wrong order
  • You need to remove a blank, duplicate, or irrelevant page from a PDF
  • You're assembling a document and need to rearrange sections visually
  • You want to see every page at a glance before deciding what to keep

Why use Orisod for this?

You see an actual thumbnail of every page, not just a page number, which makes it much easier to reorder or delete the right ones. Everything happens locally in your browser using PDF.js and pdf-lib — your document is never uploaded to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Does this affect text selectability?
No, the actual PDF page content is preserved exactly — only the order and which pages are included change.
Can I delete more than one page?
Yes, click the ✕ on as many pages as you want to remove before saving.
What if I want to undo a deletion?
Reload the page and upload your PDF again to start over from the original.
Is my PDF uploaded to a server?
No, the entire process happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.
