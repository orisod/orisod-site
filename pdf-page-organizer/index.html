PDF Page Organizer

Drag page thumbnails into a new order, remove unwanted pages and download the rebuilt PDF.

🔒 Processed on your device · No upload · No account · No watermark
Drag any page thumbnail to reorder it. Click the ✕ on a page to remove it.

Organize the document from page thumbnails

Every page appears as a thumbnail that you can drag into a new sequence or remove before export. The tool rebuilds a PDF from the pages you keep without intentionally rasterizing their visible content. Ordinary text can remain selectable, but bookmarks, links, forms, annotations, page labels and digital signatures may change or become invalid.

When a visual page overview helps

  • Correct pages scanned in the wrong sequence
  • Remove blank, duplicate or irrelevant PDF pages
  • Rearrange document sections using a visual overview
  • Inspect every page before deciding what to keep

Frequently asked questions

Does this affect text selectability?
PDF pages are copied without intentional rasterization, so ordinary selectable text can retain that property. The document structure is rebuilt, however, and interactive elements or signatures may not survive unchanged.
Can I delete more than one page?
Yes. Remove as many thumbnails as needed, provided at least one page remains. Review the complete sequence before saving.
What if I want to undo a deletion?
Before export, reload and select the original PDF again to reset the session. After export, keep the original file as the reliable way to recover a deleted page.
Is my PDF uploaded to a server?
No. JavaScript renders the thumbnails and rebuilds the PDF locally.
