What is a QR code?

A QR code is a square barcode that stores text or a link, which any phone camera can scan to open instantly. They're commonly used on business cards, posters, menus, and product packaging to send people to a website or piece of information without typing anything.

When should you use this tool?

  • You want to share a link on a printed flyer, poster, or business card
  • You're setting up a Wi-Fi network name/password, contact info, or event link people can scan
  • You need a quick way to transfer a short piece of text between devices
  • You want a custom-colored QR code to match your branding

Why use Orisod for this?

The QR code is generated entirely in your browser — the text or link you type is never sent to a server. You can choose the output size and color before downloading as a PNG image.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit to how much text I can encode?
QR codes can hold a few thousand characters, but shorter content (like a URL) scans faster and more reliably. Very long text may produce a denser, harder-to-scan code.
Do QR codes expire?
No, a QR code generated here never expires — it's a static image encoding your exact text, not a redirect service.
Is what I type sent to a server?
No, the QR code is generated locally in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you enter is transmitted anywhere.
Can I change the color of the QR code?
Yes, use the color picker before generating. For best scan reliability, keep good contrast against a light background.
