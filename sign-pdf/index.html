What does this tool do?

This tool lets you draw a signature with your mouse or finger, then click anywhere on a chosen PDF page to place it. A live preview shows exactly where the signature will land before you download the signed file.

When should you use this tool?

  • You need to sign a contract, form, or agreement without printing and scanning it
  • You're returning a signed document quickly by email
  • You want a simple, no-account way to add a signature to a one-off PDF
  • You're filling out a form that just needs a signature, not a full e-signature workflow

Frequently asked questions

Is this a legally binding e-signature?
This tool draws and embeds an image of your signature onto the PDF — it doesn't provide cryptographic signing, identity verification, or an audit trail like dedicated e-signature services. For documents that require legal e-signature compliance, use a service built for that purpose.
Can I sign more than one page?
You can place your signature on one page per pass. To sign multiple pages, run the tool again on the downloaded file and place it on a different page.
Can I resize my signature after placing it?
Yes, use the width slider before downloading — the preview updates instantly so you can see the new size before committing.
Is my PDF uploaded to a server?
No, the entire process happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.
