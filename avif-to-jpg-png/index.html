What is an AVIF file?

AVIF is a newer image format that typically achieves smaller file sizes than JPG or WEBP at similar quality, and it's increasingly used by websites for faster loading. However, some older software, design tools, and platforms still don't support opening AVIF files directly, which is why converting to JPG or PNG is sometimes necessary.

When should you use this tool?

  • You downloaded an AVIF image and your photo editor won't open it
  • A platform or form only accepts JPG or PNG uploads
  • You need to share the image with someone whose device or software doesn't support AVIF
  • You want to edit the image in a tool that doesn't yet support the format

Why use Orisod for this?

Frequently asked questions

Why isn't my AVIF file converting?
This tool relies on your browser's built-in ability to decode AVIF. Most current versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge support it — if conversion fails, try updating your browser.
Should I choose JPG or PNG?
Choose PNG if your AVIF has transparency you want to keep, or JPG for smaller file size and maximum compatibility.
Are my files uploaded anywhere?
No, the conversion happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.
Can I convert multiple AVIF files at once?
Yes, select or drag multiple files and download them individually or all together.
