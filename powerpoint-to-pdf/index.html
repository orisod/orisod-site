PowerPoint to PDF

Turn the visible text in a .pptx presentation into a simple PDF outline, processed locally in your browser.

ℹ️ Text-only conversion: this pulls the title and body text from each slide, in reading order, and lays it out as a simple outline in the PDF: one page per slide with text. It does not preserve slide design, images, backgrounds, charts, or layout, and speaker notes are not included. Think of this as an outline export, not a visual copy of your slides. Only the modern .pptx format is supported. The older .ppt format is not.
🔒 Processed on your device · No upload · No account · No watermark

A readable outline of the words on each slide

The converter reads visible text from a PowerPoint .pptx file and arranges it as a straightforward PDF outline. Each slide containing text gets its own page. Titles, body copy and bullet points are included according to the reading order available in the file; images, backgrounds, charts, transitions and speaker notes are not. The result is therefore useful for reading and searching, not as a visual copy of the presentation.

When the message matters more than the slide design

  • Read a deck’s written content without opening PowerPoint
  • Search presentation text in a simpler document
  • Share an outline when slide visuals are unnecessary
  • Extract the words without uploading the presentation

Frequently asked questions

Will the PDF look like my actual slides?
No. The PDF is a text outline, not a rendered copy of the slides. It excludes slide design, images, backgrounds, charts and layout.
Are speaker notes included?
No. The converter extracts visible slide text but does not include speaker notes.
Does this work with the older .ppt format?
Only .pptx files are supported. Open an older .ppt file in compatible presentation software and save a .pptx copy before using this converter.
Is my presentation uploaded to a server?
No. JavaScript reads the presentation and creates the PDF inside your browser.
