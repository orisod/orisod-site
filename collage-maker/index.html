Start with 2 to 9 photos and choose a grid for that number. You can reorder the images, zoom or reposition each one inside its cell, and apply filters individually. Square, vertical story, A4 and custom canvas sizes are available. Add a short caption if needed, then export the finished collage as PNG or JPG at your chosen resolution.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a watermark on the downloaded collage?
No. The exported PNG or JPG does not include an Orisod watermark, logo or other added branding.
Do filters apply to the whole collage or each photo separately?
Adjustments are applied per photo. Select a cell, choose its filter and repeat for any other image you want to change. The photos do not have to use the same filter.
What's the maximum number of photos?
You can include up to nine photos. The tool provides layouts for every count from 2 through 9, with more than one arrangement for many counts.
Can I use a background image instead of a solid color?
No. This version uses a solid background color selected with the color picker.
Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. JavaScript processes the photos and builds the final collage in your browser. The files are not sent to a server for this task.
