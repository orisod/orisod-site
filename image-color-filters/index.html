What does this tool do?

This tool applies simple, classic photo adjustments: converting to black & white, applying a warm sepia tone, and fine-tuning brightness and contrast — all with an instant preview so you can see the result before downloading.

When should you use this tool?

  • You want a black & white version of a photo for a specific look
  • You're going for a vintage, warm-toned sepia effect
  • A photo came out too dark, too flat, or needs a quick brightness/contrast fix
  • You want to prepare an image with a consistent style across a set of photos

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine a filter with brightness/contrast changes?
Yes, the filter and the sliders apply together — for example, black & white with increased contrast.
Does this reduce image quality?
No, these are color adjustments applied to the full-resolution image — no compression or downscaling happens as part of the filter.
Is my photo uploaded anywhere?
No, the entire process happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.
Can I undo the filter after applying it?
Select "Original" and reset the sliders to 0 to return to the unedited image, or re-upload the file to start over.
