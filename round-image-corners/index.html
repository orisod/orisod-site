What does this tool do?

This tool masks your image into either rounded rectangle corners or a perfect circle, making everything outside that shape transparent. It's the standard look for profile pictures, avatars, and app icons across most platforms.

When should you use this tool?

  • You need a circular profile picture for social media, Slack, or a messaging app
  • You're designing a UI and want a consistent rounded-corner style for thumbnails
  • You want an image with a transparent background shaped like a circle or rounded square
  • You're preparing an app icon or badge with soft corners

Why use Orisod for this?

Switch between rounded corners (with an adjustable radius) and a full circle crop, with a live preview before downloading. The output is a transparent PNG, ready to drop onto any background. Everything happens locally in your browser — your photo is never uploaded to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Will the background be transparent?
Yes, the area outside the selected shape (circle or rounded corners) becomes fully transparent in the downloaded PNG.
Can I control how rounded the corners are?
Yes, use the radius slider to adjust from barely-rounded to almost-circular corners.
Does the circle crop use the full image?
The circle is based on the smaller of the image's width or height, centered — so if your photo isn't square, some of the longer side will be cropped out.
Is my photo uploaded anywhere?
No, the entire process happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.
