About Orisod

Free, simple, and privacy-friendly tools for images and PDFs.

What we do

Orisod Labs builds free online tools that help you convert, compress, resize, crop, and optimize images and PDFs — no accounts, no installs, no unnecessary friction. Each tool is designed to solve one specific task, quickly and for free.

Why build another page of tools that already exist?

We didn't reinvent the wheel. Plenty of image and PDF converters already exist online — we know, and you've probably used one. What we did was put 40 tools in one place, so you don't have to jump between different sites every time you need to convert, compress, or edit something. Everything runs in your browser, no accounts, no ads hiding the real button, and your files never leave your device. Fast, simple, secure, and free — that's it.

Our approach to privacy

Every Orisod tool runs entirely inside your browser. When you convert or edit a file, it's processed on your own device using JavaScript — it's never uploaded to our servers or seen by anyone else. You could disconnect from the internet halfway through a conversion and the tool would keep working.

🔒 We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which tools are most useful — but this only tracks anonymous usage patterns (like page visits), never the content of the files you process. See our Privacy Policy for more details.

What's next

We're actively adding new tools and improving existing ones based on what people actually need. If there's a tool you wish existed, or something that could work better, we'd love to hear about it.
