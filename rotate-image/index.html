Rotate Image

Rotate or flip your image instantly. Everything happens locally in your browser — your file is never uploaded anywhere.


Supports JPG, PNG, WEBP and other common image formats.

What does rotating or flipping an image mean?

Rotating turns the image around its center by a fixed angle (90°, 180°, or 270°), while flipping mirrors it horizontally or vertically. Both are common fixes for photos that were taken sideways, upside down, or need to be mirrored for a specific layout.

When should you use this tool?

  • A photo from your phone or camera appears sideways or upside down
  • You need a mirrored version of an image for a design or print layout
  • A scanned document came out rotated and needs correcting
  • You want to quickly fix orientation before using the image elsewhere

Why use Orisod for this?

You can combine multiple rotations and flips before downloading — for example, rotate 90° and then flip horizontally — and preview the result instantly. Everything runs locally in your browser using the Canvas API, so your photo is never uploaded to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Does rotating reduce image quality?
No, rotating and flipping by 90°/180° increments doesn't lose any image data or quality.
Can I combine a rotation with a flip?
Yes, apply as many rotations and flips as you need before downloading — each one applies on top of the last.
Can I undo a rotation?
Use the "Reset" button to return to the original image and start over.
Is my image uploaded anywhere?
No, the entire process happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.
