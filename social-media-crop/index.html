Match the crop to the destination

Social posts, stories, thumbnails and cover images use different aspect ratios. A preset locks the crop to the selected ratio and then resizes the result to its configured pixel dimensions. Platforms can change their recommendations and may crop images differently across devices, so keep important text and faces away from the edges and verify current requirements for critical work.

When a platform preset saves time

  • Frame a photograph for an Instagram post, TikTok video cover or YouTube asset
  • Replace an awkward automatic crop with one you control
  • Prepare a banner or cover image using a listed preset
  • Create separate versions of one photograph for platforms with different ratios

Frequently asked questions

Will my image be stretched to fit the preset?
No. The selection retains the preset aspect ratio, then the chosen area is resized to the configured pixel dimensions. This avoids stretching, although resizing can still soften a small source image.
What if my photo is too small for the preset's pixel size?
The crop keeps the requested ratio and the export scales it to the preset dimensions. Significant enlargement cannot add real detail, so a small source may appear soft or pixelated.
Can I use this for platforms not listed?
The presets cover common formats. For another ratio or dimensions, use the regular Crop Image tool and confirm the current requirements with the destination platform.
Is my image uploaded anywhere?
No. JavaScript performs the crop and resize locally in your browser.
