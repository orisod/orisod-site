What does this tool do?

Every social platform expects a different image size and aspect ratio — a square for an Instagram post, a tall 9:16 frame for Stories, a wide banner for LinkedIn. This tool locks your crop selection to the exact ratio each platform needs, then resizes the result to that platform's recommended pixel dimensions, so your image looks right instead of getting stretched or awkwardly cropped after upload.

When should you use this tool?

  • You're posting a photo to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or another platform and want it to display correctly
  • You noticed your uploaded image looks cropped oddly or blurry because the platform resized it automatically
  • You're creating a banner or cover photo and need the exact required dimensions
  • You want to batch-prepare the same photo for multiple platforms with different ratios

Why use Orisod for this?

Pick a platform preset first, then your crop selection automatically locks to that exact ratio as you drag — no guessing or manual math. The final image is resized to the platform's standard pixel dimensions. Everything happens locally in your browser, so your photo is never uploaded to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Will my image be stretched to fit the preset?
No, the selection area itself is locked to the correct ratio while you drag, so nothing gets stretched — only the exact area you select is used, then resized to the platform's standard pixel size.
What if my photo is too small for the preset's pixel size?
The tool will still crop to the correct ratio and scale up to the target size, though very small source images may look softer when enlarged significantly.
Can I use this for platforms not listed?
These presets cover the most common formats. For a custom ratio, use the regular Crop Image tool instead.
Is my image uploaded anywhere?
No, the entire process happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.
