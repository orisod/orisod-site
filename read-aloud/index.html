Read Aloud

Paste your text below and have it read aloud right here in your browser — pick a voice, adjust speed and pitch, and follow along as each sentence is highlighted.

🔒 Processed on your device · Never sent anywhere · Not stored
What this actually is: Read Aloud uses your browser's own built-in text-to-speech engine to read your text out loud, live, right on this page. It cannot generate or export a downloadable audio file (an MP3, for example) — the browser's underlying Web Speech API only supports live playback, not audio export. Which voices are available depends entirely on your device and browser, not on Orisod — a phone, a Mac, and a Windows PC will typically offer different voices, and we can't add, guarantee, or improve any specific voice or language that isn't already installed on your system.
Not supported in this browser. Read Aloud needs the Web Speech API, which this browser doesn't provide. Try a recent version of Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.

What does this tool do?

Read Aloud speaks any text you paste in, using your browser's own built-in text-to-speech voices. As it reads, the current sentence is highlighted so you can follow along visually while you listen. Everything — splitting the text, picking a voice, and generating the speech itself — happens locally in your browser via the Web Speech API. Nothing you type or paste is ever sent to a server.

When should you use this tool?

  • Proofreading a draft — hearing your own writing out loud makes awkward phrasing and typos much easier to catch
  • Listening to a long article, script, or set of notes while doing something else
  • Practicing pronunciation while learning a language
  • Making written content more accessible for anyone who prefers or needs to listen instead of read

Frequently asked questions

Can I download the speech as an audio file?
No. The Web Speech API that powers this tool only supports live, in-browser playback — it doesn't provide any way to save what it reads as an MP3, WAV, or any other audio file. That's a limitation of the underlying browser technology, not something Orisod can add.
Why do I only see a few voices, or why do they sound robotic?
Every voice listed comes from your own device and browser, not from Orisod — we don't ship or install any voices. macOS and iOS tend to include several natural-sounding voices out of the box; other setups may offer fewer, more robotic-sounding options. The set you see is entirely up to your operating system.
Is my text ever sent to a server?
No. Reading your text, including generating the speech itself, happens entirely on your device through your browser's built-in speechSynthesis API. Nothing is uploaded.
Why did playback stop partway through a long text?
Text is automatically split into sentence-sized pieces before being read, which works around a known bug in some browsers where very long blocks of text cut off partway through. If playback still stops early, try pressing Play again or switching to a different installed voice.
