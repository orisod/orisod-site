Remove Blank Pages from PDF

Find the blank pages in a PDF, check each suggestion and download a copy without them.

Higher sensitivity allows pages with small marks or light scan noise to be marked as blank. Review the marked pages before saving.

This PDF has a digital signature. Removing pages changes the downloaded copy, so the signature may no longer be valid.

This is a large PDF, so checking it may take a while and use a lot of memory. If the browser slows down, close other tabs.

    Removing pages may affect bookmarks, internal links, forms, tags, attachments, PDF/A compliance and digital signatures. Check the downloaded PDF before replacing the original.

    How it works

    1. Add a PDF to preview its pages.
    2. Review the pages marked as blank.
    3. Select the pages you want to remove, then save the PDF.

    Help and details

    Help and common issues

    • Add a PDF by clicking the box or dragging the file onto it. Each page is checked in your browser and shown as a small preview.
    • Pages that look blank are marked for removal. Check every marked page: a page can look empty in a small preview and still matter.
    • Clear Remove page on any page you want to keep, or tick it on a page you want to remove. Start over resets the tool so you can add another PDF.
    • Large PDFs take longer and use more memory. If the browser slows down, close other tabs or split the PDF first.
    • Removing pages may affect bookmarks, internal links, forms, tags, attachments, PDF/A compliance and digital signatures. Check the downloaded PDF before replacing the original.

    Privacy and sharing

    • Your PDF is processed in your browser and is not uploaded to Orisod servers.
    • This page doesn't save your PDF, its name or your choices. Closing the tab removes them from the page.
    • The share button includes only this tool's address, not your file or the pages you chose.

    What blank-page detection means

    Each page is drawn as a small picture and compared with the colour of its own paper, so off-white and grey scans are evaluated relative to their background. Marks clearly darker than the paper are counted, apart from a thin band along the edges where scanners leave shadows. When those marks cover almost none of the page, it's suggested as blank. The sensitivity sets how dark a mark must be, how much of the page it may cover and how wide the edge band is. A page with text you can't see, comments, form fields or a signature field is not suggested automatically, even if it looks empty, because that content may matter.

    When to use it

    • Clean up a scan from a document feeder that also captured the empty backs of single-sided pages
    • Remove separator pages or empty pages left at the end of a report
    • Tidy a PDF before printing, so no paper is spent on empty sheets
    • Prepare a file for a form or portal that limits how many pages you can upload

    Frequently asked questions

    Why was a page with only a page number marked as blank?
    At Balanced and More aggressive, a few small marks such as a page number or a short footer count as too little content. Choose Conservative to keep those pages, or clear Remove page before saving.
    Why wasn't a scanned blank page detected?
    Scans often have specks, shadows, a grey tint or text showing through from the other side. At Conservative those marks are enough for a page to count as having content. Try Balanced or More aggressive, or tick Remove page on it yourself.
    Can I change which pages are removed?
    Yes. The detection only makes suggestions. Tick or clear Remove page on any page. Your own choices stay as they are when you change the sensitivity.
    Will bookmarks, links and forms be preserved?
    Not always. Content, website links and form fields on the pages you keep are generally retained. Bookmarks and internal links that pointed to a removed page may stop working, and tags, attachments, PDF/A compliance and digital signatures can be affected. Check the downloaded PDF before replacing the original.
    Can it open a password-protected PDF?
    No. Remove the password in a trusted PDF application first, then add the PDF here. This tool doesn't try to unlock files.
    Is my PDF uploaded to Orisod?
    No. Your PDF is processed in your browser and is not uploaded to Orisod servers. The tool loads its PDF libraries from orisod.com.
