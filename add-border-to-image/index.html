Add Border/Frame to Image

Add a colored border around your photo — adjust the thickness, color, and rounding to taste. Everything happens locally in your browser.

🔒 Processed on your device · No upload · No account · No watermark

Supports JPG, PNG, WEBP and other common image formats.

What does this tool do?

This tool adds a solid-colored border around your image, with adjustable thickness and optional rounded outer corners. It's a simple way to give a photo a framed, polished look before sharing or printing.

When should you use this tool?

  • You want a clean frame around a photo before posting it
  • You're preparing images for a printed collage or photo album
  • You need consistent, uniform borders across a set of images
  • You want to add brand-colored padding around a product photo

Why use Orisod for this?

Adjust the border width, color, and corner rounding with a live preview before downloading. Everything happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API — your photo is never uploaded to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Does the border change the image's original dimensions?
Yes, the border is added around the image, so the final output is larger than the original by the border width on each side.
Can I make the border transparent?
Currently the border is always a solid color you choose. For a transparent-background image, use our Round Image Corners tool instead.
Is my image uploaded anywhere?
No, the entire process happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.
What formats does this support?
JPG, PNG, WEBP and other common image formats your browser can read.
