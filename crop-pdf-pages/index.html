What does cropping a PDF do?

Cropping trims the visible area of every page by the amount you specify on each side, measured in millimeters. The underlying content isn't deleted or redrawn — the page's visible boundary (its crop box) is simply moved inward, hiding the margins you no longer want to show or print.

When should you use this tool?

  • A scanned document has wide, uneven margins you want to remove
  • You're preparing a PDF for a print layout that requires specific margins
  • A page was exported with extra white space you want to trim off
  • You want to remove a header or footer band that sits within a fixed margin

Why use Orisod for this?

You enter exact millimeter measurements for each side and every page in the PDF is cropped consistently in one pass. The whole process happens locally in your browser using pdf-lib — your document is never uploaded to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping delete any content permanently?
No, it moves the page's visible boundary — the content underneath isn't redrawn or rasterized, so text stays selectable.
Can I crop different amounts from each side?
Yes, the top, bottom, left and right margins are independent — set any of them to 0 to leave that side untouched.
What if my PDF has pages of different sizes?
The same margin measurements are applied to every page. If a page is too small for the margins you entered, you'll see an error asking you to reduce them.
Is my PDF uploaded to a server?
No, the entire process happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.
