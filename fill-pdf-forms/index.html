Fill PDF Forms

Upload a fillable PDF and type directly into its real text boxes, checkboxes, radio buttons, and dropdowns — positioned right on the page — then download a flattened copy.

🔒 Processed on your device · No upload · No account
Fillable field Read-only / not supported

What does this tool do?

This tool reads the real, interactive fields built into a PDF form — the same fields Adobe Acrobat or a browser's built-in PDF viewer would let you click into — and turns them into editable boxes positioned directly over the rendered page. Type your answers, check your boxes, pick your dropdown values, then click "Flatten & Download" to bake everything into a normal, non-editable PDF page (real vector text, not a rasterized screenshot).

When should you use this tool?

  • You received a fillable PDF form (application, intake form, government form) and need to fill it out without printing it
  • You want to fill in a form on your phone or a computer without Adobe Acrobat installed
  • You want the finished form locked down as a flat PDF before emailing it, so the recipient can't accidentally change your answers

Frequently asked questions

What if my PDF has no fillable fields?
Some PDFs only look like forms — lines and boxes drawn to resemble one, with no real interactive fields underneath. This tool detects that case honestly and tells you no fillable fields were found, rather than guessing at box positions. If that happens, the file isn't a real AcroForm and can't be filled this way.
Does this support digital signature fields?
No. Digital signature fields are a different technology (cryptographic signing) and aren't supported here — they're shown grayed out on the page so you know they exist. If you just need to place a drawn or uploaded signature image, use our Sign PDF tool instead.
Will calculated fields or form validation still work?
No. Some PDF forms use embedded JavaScript to auto-calculate totals, validate entries, or enforce "choose only one" rules across a set of checkboxes. This tool doesn't run any embedded PDF scripting, so those behaviors won't happen automatically — double-check your entries before downloading.
Is my PDF uploaded to a server?
No, the entire process — reading the form, filling it in, and flattening it — happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.
