Delete PDF Pages

Type the page numbers or ranges you want to remove, then download the rest — no need to click through thumbnails one by one. Everything happens locally in your browser.

🔒 Processed on your device · No upload · No account · No watermark

What does this tool do?

Instead of clicking through each page one at a time, this tool lets you type the exact page numbers or ranges you want removed — like "2, 4, 7-9" — and instantly see how many pages will be deleted before you download the result. It's built for PDFs where you already know which pages need to go.

When should you use this tool?

  • You need to remove a handful of pages from a long document quickly
  • A scanned PDF has blank or duplicate pages at known positions
  • You want to strip out a range of pages, like an appendix or a chapter
  • You already know the page numbers and don't need a visual thumbnail picker

Why use Orisod for this?

Type page numbers and ranges separated by commas, and see a live preview of exactly how many pages will be deleted and how many will remain before you commit. The whole process happens locally in your browser using pdf-lib — your document is never uploaded to a server. Prefer to see thumbnails and delete pages visually instead? Try the PDF Page Organizer.

Frequently asked questions

How do I specify which pages to delete?
Type page numbers separated by commas, and use a hyphen for ranges — for example "2, 4, 7-9" deletes pages 2, 4, 7, 8 and 9.
Can I delete every page?
No, at least one page must remain in the document. You'll see an error if your input would remove all pages.
Does this preserve the quality of the remaining pages?
Yes, the remaining pages are copied over exactly as they were — nothing is rasterized or recompressed.
Is my PDF uploaded to a server?
No, the entire process happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.
